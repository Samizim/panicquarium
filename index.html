About the game

Your fish are hungry. The Deep is hungrier.

Panicquarium! is a colorful aquarium action game by Studio Zim, a tiny studio in Brazil. Drop food, watch your fish grow, scoop up the sand dollars they drop and spend them on more fish, tastier food and bigger zappers.

But keep one eye on the water: greedy invaders from the Deep are on an all-you-can-eat buffet tour, and your fish are on the menu!

How to play

  1. Tap the water to drop food. Well-fed fish grow up.
  2. Tap the sand dollars your fish drop.
  3. Buy more fish, better food and zappers for the tank.
  4. Invader spotted? Bonk it until it gives up.
  5. Buy all three pieces of the treasure chest to clear the level.

Game modes

Three ways to make a splash

Adventure

Story Mode

25 hand-made tanks across five worlds, a silly story, boss fights, each with a big "Boss ahead!" face-off, and a treasure chest to crack open in every tank. Unlock 30 tank buddies (and a few secret ones), each with its own trick.

Roguelite

Deep Dive

Dive from your boat to the sea floor on a side-view Sea Map: The Shallows, The Twilight Zone and The Abyss, with a boss waiting at the bottom. Your whole school dives with you, every tank has a twist, Lucky Trinkets bend the rules, and the tide waits for no fish: win before it goes out!

Cozy

My Fishbowl

Your own cozy tank, no invaders allowed. Name, dye and dress up your fish, decorate, raise babies, meet visitors and collect daily gifts, even offline. A little Auto Feeder robot even helps at snack time.
