*Last updated: 11 November 2026. Developer: Studiozim ([studiozim.com.br](https://studiozim.com.br)).*

Panicquarium! is a casual aquarium game for teens and adults (13+). We don't run any servers and we don't collect your personal information ourselves. On phones, the free version shows ads through Google AdMob, and that's the one place data leaves your device. This page explains what goes where.

## What the game stores

- **Your progress, on your device only:** levels reached, shells, your fish and fishbowl, settings and the player names you type. These stay on your phone or computer. We never receive them.
- **Backups you make yourself:** the "Back up" button copies your save as a code to your clipboard (and, on PC, a file in the game's save folder). Where you paste or keep that code is up to you.
- **An error log on your device** (`errors.log` in the game's save folder) that notes crashes and errors. It never leaves your device unless you choose to send it to us.

## Ads (Android and iOS only)

The free mobile version shows ads: ones you choose to watch for a reward, and a few full-screen ones between levels. They're served by **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads, and to stop ad fraud, AdMob may collect:

- your device's **advertising ID**;
- your **IP address** and the rough location it gives (country or city, never your exact location);
- **device information** (model, operating system, language);
- **how you interact with ads** (views, taps) and basic app performance data.

Google handles that data under its own policy: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Your choices:**

- **Consent:** where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and Brazil), the game asks before showing personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time with **Options > Ad privacy choices**.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset it or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
- **Remove Ads:** buying Remove Ads turns ads off for good, and with them AdMob's data collection.

The Steam (PC) version has no ads.

## Purchases

In-game purchases (Remove Ads, Starter Bundle, decoration packs) are paid through **Google Play**, the **App Store** or **Steam**. They process the payment under their own privacy policies. We never see your card or payment details. The game only receives a confirmation of what you bought, so it can unlock it.

## Notifications (Android)

If you allow it, the game schedules reminders **on your device** (your daily gift, your fishbowl's piggy banks). They're created and shown by your phone. Nothing is sent to us, and you can turn them off in your phone's settings.

## What the game does not do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics of our own, and we never sell your data.
- No chat or contact with other players. The Daily Dive "share" button only copies a line of text for you to paste wherever you like.
- No exact location, camera, microphone or contacts.

## Store services

If you play through a store (Google Play, Steam), that store may handle achievements, leaderboards or cloud saves under its own privacy policy.

## Children

Panicquarium! is not made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we'll help.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the LGPD in Brazil), you may have the right to access or delete your data. We keep no data about you ourselves. For the ad data, use the choices above or [Google's privacy tools](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy). For anything else, write to [suporte@studiozim.com.br](mailto:suporte@studiozim.com.br).

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: [suporte@studiozim.com.br](mailto:suporte@studiozim.com.br).
